Data privacy & control

Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 2026

At CarCareHub, we respect your privacy and give you control over your personal information. You can request deletion of personal data associated with your account or interactions with our services.

How to Request Data Deletion

If you wish to have your personal data permanently removed from our records, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to our official support email address: carcarehub0@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: “Request for Personal Data Deletion”.
  3. Include your registered full name and phone number so we can accurately identify your account and service records.
  4. Explicitly mention that you want your CarCareHub personal data deleted.
  5. Our team will verify your identity and process the request in accordance with our privacy practices.
Send Deletion Request Email ↗

Information That May Be Deleted

Upon receiving and verifying your request, we can delete or anonymize eligible personal information, including:

  • Customer name and contact information.
  • Phone number and email address.
  • Vehicle information associated with your account (such as make, model, variant, or registration details).
  • Service booking and service history information.
  • Other personal information associated with your account, where legally permitted.

Processing Time

We will acknowledge your request and aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days, subject to applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Important Information

Certain records, including invoices, payment records and transaction information, may need to be retained where required by applicable law (such as tax, accounting, or statutory regulatory obligations). We will delete or anonymize other eligible personal information.
